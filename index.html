Why it's built this way

Made for the desk, the warehouse floor, and the airgapped network.

PeekLens Lite is a learning tool first — it explains syntax, it doesn't certify it.

Runs fully offline

No server calls, no message upload, nothing leaves your machine. Built for teams who work behind restricted or airgapped networks.

Teaches, doesn't certify

PeekLens Lite explains what a segment means and why it's structured that way. It's not an official validator, and it won't pretend to be one.

Built for the field

Trade, logistics, customs, transport and integration teams read this syntax daily. Every example comes from a message shape they'd actually see.
