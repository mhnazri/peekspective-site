PeekGarage Lite: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

PeekGarage Lite ("the app") is a vehicle logbook for iPhone, published by Peekspective.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The records you enter (vehicle details, service entries, costs, mileage and plans) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party.

Your device's own backup (for example iCloud or a computer backup), if you have enabled it, may include the app's data. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device removes the data stored by the app. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data.

External links

The app may open links in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@peekspective.com or visit peekspective.com/support.
