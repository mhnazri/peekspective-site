PEEK by PeekLens

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

PEEK by PeekLens ("PEEK" or "the app") is a free, offline EDIFACT syntax checker published by Peekspective. You can paste one complete EDIFACT interchange and check its syntax on your device.

Data we collect

PEEK does not require an account. The app is designed without analytics, advertising, tracking, or collection of your EDIFACT message content. The syntax-checking feature does not send your messages or validation results to Peekspective servers.

Data processed on your device

Text that you paste and the corresponding syntax-check results are processed locally and held in the app's temporary memory during your session. PEEK does not provide message history, cloud synchronization, file import, or automatic message uploads.

The app may save a basic preference, such as your Light, Dark, or System appearance selection, locally on your device. This preference does not contain your EDIFACT message.

Clearing your message

Selecting Clear removes the current pasted message and its associated result from the active app session. Returning from the results screen or temporarily switching away from the app may preserve your input during the same session. PEEK is designed not to retain EDIFACT messages or results as persistent app records after the app process ends.

PEEK does not intentionally access your clipboard automatically. Content enters the app when you choose to paste or type it.

Feedback and support

If you voluntarily contact us, your email provider or other communication service may transmit the contact information and message you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your inquiry. Please avoid sending confidential EDIFACT payloads or sensitive business data in support requests.

External links and app stores

If you open an external link or contact support, the destination service may apply its own privacy policy. Apple and other app-store providers may separately process information connected with downloading or using their stores; that activity is not part of PEEK's local syntax validation.

Security

Keeping EDIFACT processing on your device and not storing message history helps limit exposure. You are responsible for securing your device and deciding what information to paste. No device or software environment can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

PEEK is a technical utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at the same public policy address with an updated date.
