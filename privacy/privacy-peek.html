Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026
Publisher: Peekspective

Summary

PEEK is a free EDIFACT syntax checker that works offline. In short:

  • Your message is checked on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Nothing you paste is saved. The text and the result exist only while the app is in use.
  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The only thing PEEK remembers is your choice of appearance (light, dark or system).
  • If you contact us, we receive only what you choose to send.

About this policy

This policy explains how PEEK by PeekLens (“PEEK”, “the app”) handles information. PEEK is published by Peekspective (“we”, “us”). It applies to the app on every device and platform where it is offered.

What PEEK does

You paste or type one complete EDIFACT interchange, and PEEK reports syntax findings. It is a syntax checker only. It is not an official validator and does not confirm that a message is correct, approved or accepted by any trading partner or authority.

The check runs entirely on your device and does not need an internet connection.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store:

  • the EDIFACT text you paste or type;
  • the results of any check;
  • your name, e-mail address or any account details (PEEK has no accounts);
  • your location, contacts, photos, files or device identifiers;
  • usage statistics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

PEEK contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking components.

Information handled on your device

Message text and results. The text you paste and the result of the check are held in the app’s temporary memory while you use it. PEEK has no history, no file import or export, no cloud sync and no automatic upload. They are not written to storage by PEEK.

Appearance setting. PEEK saves one small preference on your device: whether you chose the light, dark or system appearance. It contains no message content and is not sent anywhere.

Clearing and retention

Choosing Clear removes the current text and its result from the app. Going back from the result screen to edit, or switching to another app for a short time, may keep your text during the same session. When the app is closed by you or by your device, the text and result are gone.

We hold no copy of your messages, so there is nothing for us to retain, return or delete.

Clipboard and your device

PEEK does not read your clipboard on its own. Text enters the app only when you paste or type it.

Your device and its operating system have their own features that PEEK does not control. For example, text you copy from another app may stay on the system clipboard, and the system may keep a snapshot of the app screen when you switch apps. Please manage these in line with how sensitive your data is.

Feedback and support

PEEK can open your e-mail app with a feedback message already addressed to us. The message is pre-filled only with the app version and the EDIFACT releases the app supports. It never includes your pasted text or any result.

What you send is up to you. Your e-mail provider handles the message in transit, and we use what we receive only to answer you. Please do not send confidential EDIFACT messages or sensitive business data in a support request.

Links and websites

The Privacy, Feedback and support links open outside the app, in your web browser or e-mail app. Visiting a web page is an ordinary web request. The website operator, and any service that carries the request, may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, as with any website visit. Those services have their own privacy practices.

App stores

The store you download PEEK from may process information about your download and use of the store under its own policies. That is separate from PEEK and from the syntax check.

Security

Keeping the check on your device and not storing messages limits exposure. You remain responsible for the security of your device and for what you choose to paste. No software can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

PEEK is a technical tool for professionals and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

PEEK holds no personal information about you, so there is nothing in the app to access, correct or delete. If you have e-mailed us, you may ask us to access, correct or delete that correspondence, to the extent the law where you live provides.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at the same address and update the date at the top.
