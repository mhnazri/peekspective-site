PeekLens Essential Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy applies to PeekLens Essential, the EDIFACT-focused app made available by Peekspective for iOS and Android. It is scoped to the present app and does not automatically extend to any future PeekLens product.

In short: PeekLens Essential does not transmit your EDI message content to external servers. Messages you paste, import or share into the app are processed on your device.

Overview

PeekLens Essential is an offline, syntax-only UN/EDIFACT message inspection tool for EDI professionals, consultants, implementers and support teams. This policy describes what the app does and does not do with your data, based on its implementation as of the last updated date above.

The EDI messages and files you work with

When you paste, type, import or share an EDIFACT message into the app:

  • The message is processed entirely on your device. The app does not send message content to any server operated by us or by any third party.
  • There is no backend or cloud step. Inspecting, explaining, comparing and masking a message all happen locally, using the UN/EDIFACT reference directory bundled with the app.
  • If you import a file with the system file picker, the app reads the file you select and does not upload it. If the location you choose is a cloud-linked folder you set up yourself (for example iCloud Drive or Google Drive), that transfer is between your device and your own cloud provider, under their terms. The app does not initiate it or see it.
  • If you share a message into the app from another app, the handoff happens on your device. On iOS this uses an App Group container shared with the app's Share Extension. On Android the shared content is held in memory until the app opens it. No network transfer is involved on either platform.

History (optional, off by default)

  • Off by default. History is not recorded unless you turn it on in the app.
  • Metadata only. When on, history stores the timestamp, message type, EDIFACT release, error and warning counts, and any note or bookmark you add. It does not store the raw message you inspected.
  • Encrypted at rest. History entries are kept in your device's secure storage: the Keychain on iOS and Android Keystore-backed storage on Android.
  • Stays on your device. History is never transmitted anywhere by the app.
  • You can delete it. The app has a clear-history function. On iOS, uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove Keychain data straight away, so use the in-app function when you want it gone.

Masking / redaction

The redaction feature, for hiding sensitive values such as party names or reference numbers before you share a message, works in memory on your device. Nothing is stored or transmitted beyond what you then choose to do with the result, for example sharing it yourself through your device's share sheet.

App settings

A few preferences, such as your chosen theme, whether history is on, and whether you have seen an in-app notice, are stored locally using your operating system's standard app-preference storage. They are never transmitted anywhere.

What PeekLens Essential does not do

As of the last updated date above, and based on the app's code:

  • No analytics SDK is included.
  • No crash-reporting or telemetry SDK is included.
  • No third-party advertising or tracking SDK is included.
  • The app makes no runtime network requests to any server. On Android it does not request the Internet permission.
  • The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library access, and on Android it does not request storage or media permissions. Files reach the app only through the system file picker or the share sheet.

In-app purchases

The current version has no in-app purchase functionality. If purchases are introduced, they will be processed by your platform's store (the Apple App Store or Google Play) under that store's own terms and privacy policy, and we would not receive your payment card details. This policy will be updated before or when that functionality becomes available.

Children's privacy

PeekLens Essential is a professional tool for EDI/EDIFACT practitioners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example when in-app purchases are added, this policy will be updated and the last updated date will change.
